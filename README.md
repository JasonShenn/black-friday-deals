An original Black Friday and Cyber Monday deal finder, built for the make.fun
"Build the best real Black Friday deal finder" bounty
(`black-friday-deal-finder-01`).
See the bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/black-friday-deal-finder-01

DealBeacon lists real Black Friday deals its maker found and checked on
2026-11-27, each with the product, the store, a link to the product at that
store, the sale price, the usual price, the discount, the time its price was
checked, the source where the price was read, and an honest signal saying
whether it is really a deal (a new low, a strong price, or a routine or
inflated sale, with the evidence). Search by name, filter by category, store
and discount, sort by biggest discount or lowest price — all offline, from
the site's own files.

Run: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser
(or at a phone's size). No network requests — pure static files under `app/`.
